See who leads beyond the box score.
Rank NBA players by the advanced metrics analysts actually use, read a full percentile profile in one tap, and track who is heating up or cooling off right now.

StatScout puts usage rate, true shooting, shot-zone frequency and accuracy, assist and turnover rates, rebounding shares, steal and block rates, on-court impact, and the familiar per-game totals in one fast Android app.

VISUAL PERCENTILES
Every metric displays as a horizontal percentile bar with a colored marker. The color shifts from rust (0th percentile) through sand (50th) to hardwood green (100th), so you can spot elite performers instantly. Players are ranked against their own position group, so a center's rebounding is judged against centers and a guard's playmaking against guards.

COMPLETE PLAYER PROFILES
Tap any player to see the full picture: percentile rankings across Scoring, Shooting, Playmaking, Rebounding, Defense and Impact, a switch between advanced metrics and standard season stats, and recent form game by game.

GAMES, FREE
Live scores while games are on, every final with its box score, standings with playoff and play-in lines, and StatScout Power Ratings for all 30 teams. Follow your team to pin its game to the top.

STATS THAT KEEP UP WITH THE SCHEDULE
During the season, StatScout checks for newly published box scores throughout the day and updates as soon as they arrive. Every screen shows which games the numbers include, so you always know what you are looking at.

LEADERBOARDS AND FOLLOWING
Start on familiar leaders like points per game, or switch to advanced rankings. Filter by Guards, Forwards or Centers, and search every active player without losing league rank. Keep your team and favorite players together in Following, free. Tap any row to open the full profile.
