## More info

How it's built.

### The Pitch

Box scores tell you who got the volume. They don't tell you who was efficient, who was unlucky, or who is playing better than their yardage suggests.

StatScout puts the advanced numbers (usage, true shooting, shot zones, assist and rebound rates, steals and blocks, on-court impact) next to the familiar per-game totals, and renders every one as a percentile you can read at a glance. Sort the league and tap a name to see where a player stands.

### StatScout+

Leaderboards, filters, and full percentile profiles are free. StatScout+ is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase, with a 7-day free trial on subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

**Trends.** The league ranked by who is heating up and cooling off week to week.

**Recent form.** The last 1, 2, or 4 weeks applied to any player, team, or leaderboard.

**Comparisons.** Two players side by side, or one player's seasons against each other.

**Team scouting and history.** All 30 teams, every season back to 2002-03, and all-time career percentiles.

### What Mattered

**Percentiles over raw numbers.** "91st percentile" is easier to read than "61.2% true shooting", so the color bar is the main interface.

**One tap to the profile.** Any row in any list opens the full player page.

**How stats are published.** Each new source update is computed, checked, and published in one step, then served precomputed, so the app opens fast and works the same on stadium wifi.

**History in the bundle.** Older seasons ship inside the app, which keeps year-over-year comparisons instant and offline-friendly.

## What is true shooting percentage?

True shooting percentage measures scoring efficiency by counting two-pointers, three-pointers and free throws in one number. StatScout shows it as a percentile so you can see how efficient a player is compared with others at his position.

## What is usage rate in basketball?

Usage rate estimates the share of a team's possessions a player ends with a shot, a trip to the free-throw line or a turnover while he is on the court. StatScout shows it as a percentile within the player's position group.

## How often are the NBA stats updated?

In season, the app checks for newly published box scores throughout the day and updates as soon as they arrive. Every screen shows which games the numbers include.
