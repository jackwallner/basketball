## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often is data updated?

During the season, StatScout checks for newly published box scores throughout the day and updates soon after games finish. Each screen shows which games the numbers include.

### Where does the data come from?

Our percentile rankings are calculated using public pitch-tracking data. We aggregate this data to provide a visual, easy-to-read comparison of every player in the league.

### Is my data private?

Yes. StatScout does not collect any personal information, search history, or location data. Your app preferences are stored locally on your device. [Read our Privacy Policy →](https://jackwallner.com/ios/basketball/privacy-policy.html)

### What devices are supported?

StatScout is built for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.

### Do I need an account?

No. StatScout works right out of the box with no sign-up or login required. Optional Pro purchases unlock historical and comparison features.
