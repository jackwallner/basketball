The App Store name is Basketball Next: StatScout. The app is named Hardwood StatScout in-app and StatScout on the Home Screen. It is a read-only NBA statistics viewer with no accounts or sign-in.

Core flow:
1. Launch the app and open the Stats tab for current-season player rankings.
2. Filter by position group (Guards, Forwards, Centers), choose a statistic, and open a player profile for percentile rankings, standard stats, bio, and game log.
3. Open Games for the schedule, posted scores, box scores, and advanced four-factor breakdowns.
4. Open Teams for records, standings, team ratings, and projected margins.
5. Following, player comparisons, Trends, Recent Form, and historical views are available from their tabs or player/team pages.

StatScout+ features (IAP via RevenueCat):
- Trends, Recent Form, player comparisons, team scouting, historical seasons back to 2002-03, and All-Time career percentiles
- Restore Purchases is available in Settings; purchases can be tested with an Apple sandbox account

Data: public ESPN box scores mirrored by the open-source hoopR project, refreshed throughout the day. Not affiliated with the NBA, any NBA team, or the NBPA. No league, team, or player imagery is used.

The app has no accounts, social features, live scores, play-by-play, betting odds, wagers, or fantasy team management. Game projections are informational estimates, not betting odds.
