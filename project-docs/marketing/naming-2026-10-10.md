# App Store name: Basketball: Hardwood StatScout

Decided 2026-10-10, replacing "Basketball Next: StatScout". "Next" borrowed football's
Next Gen Stats and means nothing to an NBA fan. Each fleet name nods to its sport's
reference site (Baseball Savvy for Savant, Football Next for Next Gen Stats).

homograph whose SERP is glass and window apps.

## Why this name (30/30)

- `Hardwood` is the in-app product name already, and the only winnable sport-specific term
  with traffic.
- `Basketball` in the name pairs with the subtitle's `Stats`, `Analytics` and `Advanced` for
  `basketball stats`, `basketball analytics` and `basketball advanced stats`.
- Subtitle `Advanced NBA Stats & Analytics` carries the exact phrase `nba stats`.
- `StatScout` keeps the fleet brand.
- Sport first, matching Baseball Savvy and Football Next (Jack's call over "Hardwood: Basketball StatScout").

Rejected: `Basketball Stats: StatScout` (exact-phrase win on a stat-keeper SERP whose
searchers are coaches) and any name containing "NBA".
